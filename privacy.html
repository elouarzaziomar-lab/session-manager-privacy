Session Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Session Manager does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What we access

Tab URLs and titles — only when you explicitly click "Save". Used solely to store the session locally on your device.

How data is stored

All session data is stored using chrome.storage.local, a browser-native API that keeps data exclusively on your local device. No data is ever sent to any external server.

What we do NOT do

We do not collect analytics, we do not track usage, we do not use third-party services, we do not require an account, and we do not have access to any remote server.

Permissions used

tabs — to read tab URLs and titles when saving a session, and to open tabs when restoring.

storage — to persist session data locally on your device.
