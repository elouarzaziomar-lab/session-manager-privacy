Why people leave Toby

Toby pioneered the visual "boards of tabs" experience, and for teams who live inside it, it's genuinely great. But three recurring frictions push solo users to look elsewhere:

1. The mandatory account. You sign up before you can really use it.
2. The cloud. Your collections live on Toby's servers and need a connection to load.
3. The free-tier caps. As of 2026, the free plan is limited to roughly 60 cards, 3 collections and 1 workspace; going beyond means a paid subscription.

None of those are dealbreakers if you want boards and collaboration. They're dealbreakers if you just want to save a set of tabs and get them back later, privately.

Frequently asked questions

Is Session Manager really free?

Yes — completely free, no paid tier, and no cap on the number of sessions or tabs.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and use it immediately. There's no sign-in of any kind.

Where is my data stored?

Locally, via the browser's chrome.storage.local. Nothing ever leaves your device.

Can I import my data from Toby?

Not directly — Toby doesn't export to a compatible format. Session Manager has its own JSON export/import so you can back up and move your sessions freely.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Because everything is local, it works with no connection at all.
