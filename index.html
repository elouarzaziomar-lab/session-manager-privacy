Save in one click

Name your session and save all open tabs instantly. Chrome URLs are automatically filtered out.

Restore instantly

Reopen a session in a new window or directly in your current one — your choice, every time.

Preview before restoring

Expand any session to see the full list of tabs with favicons before opening anything.

Double-click to rename any session directly in the list. Confirmed with Enter, cancelled with Escape.

Back up all sessions to a JSON file and restore them anytime — even after a full reinstall.
